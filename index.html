01 The Duo

AARON & ROBYN

Formed in a rented practice room off Vista Ave with two amps, two guitars and no intention of staying quiet. Two guitars, two voices — the ritual still starts the same way: lights down, sigil up.

A
Acoustic Guitar · Lead Vocals

AARON

Plays a well-worn acoustic through a wall of tube heat. Sings like the desert is listening.

R
Lead Guitar · Backup Vocals · Crunch

ROBYN

Plays a cherry-red SG through a wall of tube heat, answering every one of Aaron’s lines with a lead of his own, then steps up to the mic when the choruses need two voices instead of one.
