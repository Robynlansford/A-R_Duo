TWO BODIES
ONE Storm

A&R started in 2021 in a windowless practice room off Vista Avenue — two amps, two guitars, a space heater, and a pact: never add a third human, never add a backing track. Just two guitars and two voices, and it has to earn its place every night.

A
Acoustic Guitar · Lead Vocals

AARON

Grew up on a high-desert ranch with a transistor radio and one Hendrix tape. Plays a well-worn acoustic through a ’72 tube stack. Writes every lyric standing up.

R
Lead Guitar · Backup Vocals · Crunch

ROBYN

Classically trained, ritually untrained. Plays a cherry-red SG named “Vulture”, answering every one of Aaron’s lines, and steps up to the mic for the harmonies that turn choruses into altar calls. Believes every crowd has a resonant frequency — and finds it by the third song.

“Boise gave us space. The desert gave us patience. The crunch pedal gave us everything else.” — ROBYN
